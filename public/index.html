how this works

new runs one investigation: a simulated human learner asks your tutor one scoped question at a time until it can explain the idea in its own words.

full does that, then the tutor picks 3 more related concepts to investigate — a small tour of the topic.

⤵ branch under any tutor answer re-investigates that answer more deeply, growing the tree. Every turn is two real model calls (~5–7¢).

tutor style changes how the tutor answers — from highly technical to simple everyday words — and applies from the next turn, even mid-run.

learner sets how much the simulated learner already knows, from a curious novice to an expert from a neighbouring field — so its questions can keep up with the tutor. auto (the default) matches the tutor style; each investigation keeps the learner it started with.

answer parts — when an answer has distinct aspects (the why, an example, a caveat), each shows as its own labelled card under the direct answer. Tap a card to fold or unfold it — one idea at a time.

glossary — every term the learner hits gets a short definition (written the moment it appears) and lives in the tree, so it syncs and exports with it. Dotted-underlined words in answers are glossary terms — tap one to see its definition right there. anki cards downloads the defined terms as a file Anki imports directly.

🎓 quiz me — a few multiple-choice questions written from what this tree actually covered (your examples, your terms, your near-misses). Questions are written once and kept with the tree; retakes just reshuffle.

custom tutors (the ✎ next to the style picker) let you write your own: name it, describe how it should answer, and it syncs to your other devices. The base rules always stay in force.

ask the tutor yourself — the box under any conversation sends your own question with the same context. It's recorded as your turn (🧑), and the simulated learner never sees it.

Trees live in this browser. Use save .know.json to move one to another device or the CLI — the file format is identical.
